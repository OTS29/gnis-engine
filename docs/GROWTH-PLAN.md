# Growth plan

Numbers below are targets and assumptions to test, not results. Replace each with real figures from your own dashboard as soon as you have them. Reviewers and investors trust measured data far more than projections.

## Who we sell to

Independent service professionals who take bookings by message: barbers, braiders, hairdressers, cleaners, tutors, photographers, mobile mechanics. Start with one city and 2 to 3 trades, so word of mouth compounds.

## What is built to support growth

- **Profession landing pages** at `/for/<trade>` (SEO, FAQ markup, sitemap).
- **Referral links** at `/r/<code>`, with sign-ups and published sites counted in the Growth tab.
- **Seller links and QR codes** for business cards, windows and social bios.
- **Every seller's site is a billboard.** Each public site is indexable and carries its own metadata and local business markup.

## Channels and funnel (assumptions to test)

| Channel | Action | Monthly effort | Assumed visitors | Sign-up rate | Sign-ups |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local outreach | Visit 20 shops or stalls per week with a QR card | 8 hours a week | 80 | 25% | 20 |
| Instagram and TikTok | 3 short "before and after site" videos a week | 5 hours a week | 1,500 | 1.5% | 22 |
| SEO pages | 12 profession pages, one new local page a week | 3 hours a week | 400 | 3% | 12 |
| Referrals | Free month for each referred seller who publishes | automated | 150 | 8% | 12 |
| Communities (WhatsApp, Facebook groups) | Share a free site-build offer | 2 hours a week | 300 | 4% | 12 |

Total target: about 78 sign-ups a month. If 50% publish a site and 20% of those pay, that is about 8 paying sellers a month.

## Unit economics (fill in with real prices)

- Price per seller per month: P (for example 15 pounds).
- Estimated monthly churn: 6% (to be measured).
- Average lifetime: 1 / 0.06, about 16.7 months.
- Lifetime revenue per seller: P x 16.7 (about 250 pounds at 15).
- Paid acquisition: if a seller costs C to acquire, aim for lifetime revenue of at least 3 times C. Spend on paid ads only after organic channels show a stable conversion rate.
- Card payments add a platform fee on each order (`PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENT`), which grows with the seller's volume.

## 90-day milestones

| By | Target | Evidence to keep |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | 25 published sites, 150 bookings through them | Dashboard screenshots, analytics exports |
| Day 60 | 60 sites, 600 bookings, first 10 paying sellers | Stripe dashboard, seller testimonials with permission |
| Day 90 | 120 sites, 1,500 bookings, 25 paying sellers | Month-on-month growth chart, referral counts |

## Measure weekly

Visitors, sign-ups, published sites, bookings per site, repeat rate, no-show rate, share of negotiated bookings, paying sellers, churn. The Analytics tab already covers the per-site figures. Keep a simple weekly spreadsheet of the platform totals.

## Evidence pack for any application or funding case

Keep dated copies of: this plan, weekly metrics, customer quotes and letters of support, press or community mentions, receipts for marketing spend, screenshots of the product, and the AI negotiation document. Be accurate: only claim figures you can show.
